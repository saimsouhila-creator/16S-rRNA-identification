About this dataset

This public viewer presents the 16S rRNA sequence identification results provided for this study.

Interpretation: The table reports the BLAST match information contained in the source dataset, including NCBI accession, hit description, identity, E-value and alignment scores. The organism names shown are the descriptions of the corresponding BLAST hits; they should be interpreted in the context of the reported sequence similarity rather than as independent confirmation of species identity.
